About KRICHMO

100% Black-owned electrical services company established in 2021.

Our Story.

KRICHMO (PTY) LTD is a small-to-medium sized electrical company servicing commercial, residential, agricultural, and industrial projects.

Locally incorporated and registered in terms of the Companies Act in 2021, we are fully compliant with South African trading rights. We are 100% black owned and managed company; we are committed to the principles of Broad-Based Black Economic Empowerment and strive to address the challenges of youth unemployment and skills development.

We continually partner with other companies with capabilities, resources, expertise, and skills in various sectors to enable us to produce world-class work to the benefit of our clients.
